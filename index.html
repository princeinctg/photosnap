About Me

Welcome to Photosnap Weddingshot. I am Md Imran, an award-winning and internationally recognized professional photographer from Bangladesh and the founder of this company. My journey as a photographer began in 2013, and driven by a passion for capturing life's most precious moments, I founded Photosnap Weddingshot on September 14, 2014. With over a decade of experience, I have had the privilege of documenting more than 3,000 weddings, each with its own unique story. As the Chief Photographer and Team Leader at Photosnap Weddingshot, I have not only honed my own skills but have also had the honor of mentoring over 50 junior photographers, helping them achieve excellence in their craft. My specialization lies in high-volume and cross-cultural wedding documentation, where I blend artistic vision with technical precision to create innovative and compelling narratives. My dedication to the art of photography has been recognized with several prestigious awards, including being a Gold Winner at the European Photography Award (EPA) in 2024. I am also a lifetime member of the WPPB International and was honored to be certified by the celebrated Indian celebrity photographer, Joseph Radhik. Furthering my expertise, I have completed multiple online certifications, including programs from Michigan State University via Coursera. My work is more than just taking pictures; it's about telling your unique love story and preserving the enchanting elegance of your special day for a lifetime. I am deeply committed to pushing creative boundaries and delivering a final product that you will cherish forever.
